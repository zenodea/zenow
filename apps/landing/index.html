The case for reading less, but better

SummaryOriginal

Key points

  • Most saved articles are never opened. The backlog turns into guilt, not knowledge.
  • A short summary is enough to decide whether something deserves your evening.

A reading list works better as a filter than an archive. The summary tells you whether a piece is worth the full read; the few that are, you read properly.

readinghabits

How it goes

Three steps, and two of them are yours.

  1. 1

    Save it

    From the Firefox extension, your phone's share sheet, or by pasting a link. It takes a second and you can carry on.

  2. 2

    Zenow reads it

    It fetches the page, writes the key points and a few paragraphs, picks the pictures worth keeping and tags it.

  3. 3

    You read the substance

    Every article opens on its summary. One tap switches to the original, cleaned up and in the same reading font.

Your list

A reading list that reads first.

Unread comes first

Unread, All and Archive sit at the top. The list opens on what you have not read yet, and on everything once that is empty.

Search the full text

Titles, summaries and the whole article are searchable, so the thing you half remember is still findable.

Tags that stay tidy

Tags are added for you and reuse the ones you already have, instead of inventing a new one every time.

Listen instead

Any summary can be read aloud, using the voice your device already has.

A weekly digest

Once a week Zenow writes up what you saved: what the week was about, and whether it was about anything at all.

Summary or original

Articles open on the summary. If you would rather start on the original, change it once in your settings.

Installs on your phone

The web app installs like an app, and pages you have opened stay readable without a connection.

Fails gently

If a summary cannot be made, the article opens on the original instead, with a small note saying why.

Saving

Wherever the link turns up.

Firefox extension

A toolbar button, Ctrl Shift U, or right click any link. Download it.

Android

Install the site, then Share → Zenow from any app.

Paste

The + button on your list takes any link.

Research

Ask a question instead of pasting a link, and Zenow searches the web and writes up what it found.

Themes

Four themes, light and dark.

Saved to your account, so every device agrees. Pick one to see this page in it.

Your keys

You pay for your own summaries, and pick the model.

OpenRouter key

Each account brings its own OpenRouter key. No subscription in the middle, and any model it offers.

Your own worker

Give Zenow a Fly.io token and it runs a worker just for you, which sleeps when there is nothing to do.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code, run it yourself, or send a fix on GitHub.
